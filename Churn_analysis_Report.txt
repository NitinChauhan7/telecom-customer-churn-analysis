TELCO CUSTOMER CHURN ANALYSIS REPORT

1. PROJECT OVERVIEW

This project analyzes customer churn in a telecommunications company using Python and Pandas.

The objective of the analysis is to identify the major factors associated with customer churn and provide actionable business recommendations to improve customer retention.

2. DATASET OVERVIEW
Total Customers: 7043
Total Churned Customers: 1869
Overall Churn Rate: 26.54%

The dataset contains customer information related to demographics, tenure, services, contracts, payment methods, monthly charges, and churn status.

Before analysis, the TotalCharges column was converted into numeric format and missing values were handled using the median value.

3. KEY FINDINGS
3.1 Contract-Wise Churn

Month-to-month customers have the highest churn rate at 42.71%.
One-year contract customers have a churn rate of 11.27%.
Two-year contract customers have the lowest churn rate of 2.83%.

3.2 Tenure-Wise Churn

Customers with 0-12 months of tenure have the highest churn rate at 47.68%.
Churn decreases as customer tenure increases.
Customers with 49-72 months of tenure have the lowest churn rate at 9.51%.

3.3 Internet Service-Wise Churn

Fiber optic customers have the highest churn rate at 41.89%.
DSL customers have a churn rate of 18.96%.
Customers without internet service have the lowest churn rate at 7.40%.

3.4 Payment Method-Wise Churn

Electronic check users have the highest churn rate at 45.29%.
Credit card users have a churn rate of 15.24%.
Bank transfer users have a churn rate of 16.71%.
Mailed check users have a churn rate of 19.11%.

3.5 Tech Support

Customers without tech support have a churn rate of 41.64%.
Customers with tech support have a much lower churn rate of 15.17%.

3.6 Online Security

Customers without online security have a churn rate of 41.77%.
Customers with online security have a churn rate of 14.61%.

3.7 Monthly Charges

The average monthly charge for churned customers is 74.44.
The average monthly charge for non-churned customers is 61.27.

4. BUSINESS RECOMMENDATIONS

1. Reduce Month-to-Month Churn
Offer discounts or additional benefits to encourage month-to-month customers to move to one-year or two-year contracts.

2. Focus on New Customers
Customers in their first 12 months show the highest churn. A strong onboarding program, regular follow-ups, and early engagement can improve retention.

3. Improve Fiber Optic Customer Experience
Fiber optic customers have a high churn rate. The company should investigate service quality, pricing, technical issues, and customer satisfaction for this segment.

4. Promote Automatic Payment Methods
Electronic check users have the highest churn rate. The company can encourage customers to use credit card or automatic bank transfer payments through incentives.

5. Strengthen Technical Support
Customers without technical support show significantly higher churn. Offering better support plans and proactive assistance may improve retention.

6. Promote Online Security Services
Customers without online security have much higher churn. Bundling online security with internet plans may improve customer retention.

7. Monitor High Monthly Charges
Churned customers have higher average monthly charges. The company should review pricing, plan value, and personalized offers for high-paying customers.

5. CONCLUSION
The analysis shows that customer churn is strongly associated with contract type, customer tenure, internet service, payment method, technical support, online security, and monthly charges.

The highest-risk customer groups include month-to-month customers, customers with short tenure, fiber optic users, electronic check users, customers without technical support, and customers without online security.

By focusing on these high-risk segments and implementing targeted retention strategies, the company can potentially reduce customer churn and improve customer retention.
